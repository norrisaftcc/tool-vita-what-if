## Story Through Flow

These diagrams tell three interconnected stories:

1. **The Student Story**: From frustration to understanding - VITA transforms the debugging experience from a maze of confusion to a guided learning journey.

2. **The Team Story**: Productive struggle leads to breakthrough - the Tools Team's journey shows that complexity forced simplification, and that struggle was the path to clarity.

3. **The System Story**: Evolution through iteration - VITA grows from a simple single agent to a sophisticated orchestrated system, with each phase building on lessons learned.

Together, they illustrate the core message: **When we focus on outcomes over process, embrace productive struggle, and iterate based on real feedback, we create tools that truly serve their users.**
